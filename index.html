01 · Thesis

The roadmap is only as credible as the system beneath it.

I trained as a computer engineer and spent two years building regulated data pipelines for banking risk teams. That work showed me where product promises meet data quality, controls and delivery constraints.

I now work upstream in life insurance, shaping backlog and delivery decisions. The move was deliberate: I wanted to decide what gets built with a clear view of what the system can actually support.

02 · Selected work

Three problems. One operating pattern.

BI · Decision layer

From 100+ reports to one governed view.

Fragmented recurring risk reporting rebuilt as six Power BI dashboards on one data model.

−90%reporting workload

Context
More than 100 recurring reports created duplicated work and inconsistent views.
My role
I owned requirements, data modelling, ETL and production dashboards.
Decision
Consolidate repeated logic into one model and six recurring decision views.

Regulatory · COREP

Regulatory reporting without recurring manual load.

COREP (European regulatory capital reporting) moved from manual assembly to controlled SAS and SQL pipelines.

>50%efficiency gain

Context
A G-SIB needed repeatable capital reporting that could withstand supervisory review.
My role
I designed and optimised critical data pipelines for credit-risk portfolios.
Decision
Move validation, reconciliation and controls for RWA, CET1 and provisions into the pipeline.

Life insurance · Product delivery

Turning product change into release-ready work.

Life-insurance initiatives structured into a shared backlog across business, technology, operations and compliance.

One viewfor release decisions

Context
Product changes crossed business, technology, operations and compliance.
My role
I shape backlog priorities, acceptance criteria and delivery controls.
Decision
Connect user stories, acceptance criteria, UAT evidence and milestones in one delivery view.
