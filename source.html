02 · Selected work

Three problems. One operating pattern.

01

From 100+ reports to one decision layer

Context
More than 100 recurring risk reports created duplicated work and inconsistent views.
My role
I owned the BI lifecycle, from requirements and data modelling through ETL and production dashboards.

What I did

  • Mapped reporting logic and consolidated repeated definitions.
  • Designed the data model and automated refresh pipelines.
  • Released six Power BI dashboards for recurring decision workflows.

Outcome 100+ reports became six dashboards; reporting workload fell by more than 90%.

02

Regulatory reporting without recurring manual load

Context
A G-SIB needed repeatable COREP (European regulatory capital reporting) under EBA and ECB supervision.
My role
I designed and optimised critical SAS and SQL pipelines for credit-risk portfolios.

What I did

  • Automated portfolio transformations and regulatory calculations.
  • Built controls across provisions, RWA (risk-weighted assets) and CET1 (core bank capital).
  • Introduced validation, reconciliation and quality checks before supervisory submission.

Outcome Recurring manual load ended, efficiency improved by over 50%, and supervisory requirements were met.

03

A delivery system for recoveries and underwriting

Context
Core banking initiatives require coordinated decisions across business, technology, risk and operations.
My role
I own the product backlog and translate roadmap intent into delivery-ready work.

What I did

  • Write user stories and acceptance criteria in Jira.
  • Maintain Confluence documentation and run Agile/Scrum ceremonies.
  • Validate user acceptance testing (UAT) evidence before release.

Outcome Roadmap milestones and operational KPIs provide shared decision input for stakeholders.
